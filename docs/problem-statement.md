# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Skincare users who have difficulty determining whether the ingredients in their existing skincare products are compatible with their skin conditions and personal sensitivities.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Users may struggle to understand ingredient lists because they contain unfamiliar scientific names and technical information. They may also find it difficult to identify potentially irritating ingredients and assess their compatibility with their skin.

## Why does it matter?
Using ingredients that are unsuitable for an individual's skin may cause irritation or other unwanted skin reactions. Without clear and personalised information, users may struggle to make informed decisions about the skincare products they already own.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Skincare users might have a problem understanding if the ingredients in the skin care products they currently use are suitable for their skin type and personal sensitivities This can also happen when users are faced with less familiar names of ingredients and have difficulties in determining which ingredients that require further attention. Adverse effects and safety concerns of cosmetic and skin care products were mentioned in a systematic review conducted by (Alyahya et al., 2025) and it was important to know the possible concerns related to the ingredients of cosmetic and skin care products. Thus, users should have a convenient method to determine the contents of their existing products according to their individual skin types and known sensitivities.
